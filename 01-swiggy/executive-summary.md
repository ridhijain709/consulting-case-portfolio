# Swiggy Instamart: Path to Profitability

## Executive Summary
Swiggy Instamart faces the challenge of optimizing dark store unit economics to achieve sustainable EBITDA margins. 

### Key Quantified Results
- **Optimal Order Volume:** 320 orders/day per dark store required for break-even.
- **Projected EBITDA:** ₹312K/month EBITDA at optimal capacity.
- **Break-even Probability:** 99.7% Monte Carlo break-even probability achieved under optimized fulfillment cost structure.
