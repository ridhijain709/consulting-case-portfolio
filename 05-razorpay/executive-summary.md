# Razorpay: Navigating the Fintech Infrastructure Landscape

## Fintech Payment Infrastructure Analysis
Razorpay is building the financial backbone for Indian businesses, capturing significant market share in the payment gateway space.

## Unit Economics & Scale
- **Total Payment Volume (TPV):** ₹17.5 Lakh Crore in FY2024
- **Take Rate:** ~0.25%
- **MDR Regulation Impact Analysis:** Zero MDR on UPI has compressed margins, necessitating cross-selling of software (RazorpayX, payroll) to drive profitability.
